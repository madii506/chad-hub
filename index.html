04 — FAQ

Questions chads ask.

What is CHAD?

A copy terminal for Hyperliquid. It reads the exchange's public leaderboard of about 48,000 traders, shows you what any of them holds right now, and copies their positions into your account, or the exact opposite.

Does CHAD hold my money?

No. Your USDC stays in your own Hyperliquid account. There is no CHAD account and no deposit to us. Orders go from your browser straight to Hyperliquid.

What is the session key?

Hyperliquid lets you approve an "agent" key that can place and cancel orders for your account but can never withdraw. CHAD creates one in your browser, your wallet approves it once, and it stays in this browser only.

What does Tail do, exactly?

It opens the same positions he has, scaled by your account size against his, then by the size slider. Max per position and the leverage cap keep it inside your limits. Anything under Hyperliquid's $10 minimum is skipped.

And Fade?

The same sizes, the other side. When the worst trader of the month is long, you are short.

How does follow mode work?

Every few seconds it reads his positions again and moves yours to match: opens what he opens, cuts what he cuts, closes what he closes. It runs in this tab, so it stops when you close it. Positions you already had in other coins are never touched.

What does it cost?

Hyperliquid's trading fees, the same as on its own site. CHAD adds nothing.

What can go wrong?

A chad can have a bad month. Copies fill at your price, not his, a moment later. Leverage can be liquidated. Size it like a chad, not like a virgin.
